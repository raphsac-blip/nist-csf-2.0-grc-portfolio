# Sample Information Security Policy

## Maryknoll Sisters — Independent Educational Simulation

**Policy Status:** Proposed educational template
**Framework:** NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

## 1. Purpose

This sample policy establishes cybersecurity
requirements for a hypothetical faith-based
nonprofit environment.

Its purpose is to protect organizational
information, reduce cybersecurity risks,
and promote responsible technology use.

## 2. Scope

This simulated policy applies to hypothetical
employees, volunteers, contractors, information
systems, and organizational data.

## 3. Access Control

- Grant users only the permissions required
  for their responsibilities.
- Review user access quarterly.
- Promptly disable accounts when personnel leave.

## 4. Authentication Security

- Require unique user accounts.
- Enable multifactor authentication (MFA).
- Prohibit sharing passwords and credentials.

## 5. Data Protection

- Restrict access to sensitive information.
- Encrypt sensitive data where appropriate.
- Protect donor, employee, and financial records.

## 6. System Security

- Apply security updates promptly.
- Maintain endpoint protection software.
- Prevent unauthorized software installation.

## 7. Incident Reporting

- Report suspicious emails and security
  incidents promptly to designated IT personnel.
- Document reported incidents and actions taken.

## 8. Backup and Recovery

- Maintain protected backups of critical data.
- Test backup restoration procedures regularly.
- Verify backup integrity before recovery.

## 9. Security Awareness

- Provide regular cybersecurity awareness training.
- Conduct simulated phishing exercises.

## 10. Governance and Review

Simulated Policy Owner: IT / GRC Department

Review Frequency: Annually or following
significant organizational changes.

Related Risk Register Entries: R-001 to R-005

## Disclaimer

This document is an independent educational
simulation inspired by Maryknoll Sisters.

It is not an official policy of Maryknoll Sisters
and has not been approved or implemented by
the organization.

No actual organizational systems or confidential
information were examined or used.
