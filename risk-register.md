# Cybersecurity Risk Register

## Maryknoll Sisters — Simulated Case Study

### Purpose

This risk register documents hypothetical
cybersecurity risks, their likelihood,
potential impact, and remediation priorities.

### Framework Alignment

NIST CSF 2.0
Function: Identify (ID)
Category: Risk Assessment (ID.RA)

### Risk Scoring Methodology

**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**

**Likelihood Scale**
- 1 = Rare
- 2 = Unlikely
- 3 = Possible
- 4 = Likely
- 5 = Almost Certain

**Impact Scale**
- 1 = Insignificant
- 2 = Minor
- 3 = Moderate
- 4 = Major
- 5 = Severe

**Risk Classification**
- 1–5 = Low
- 6–12 = Medium
- 13–19 = High
- 20–25 = Critical

| Risk ID | Threat | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Score | Priority |

| R-001 | Phishing | 4 | 4 | 16 | High |

| R-005 | Data Loss / Backup Failure | 3 | 5 | 15 | High |

### Preliminary Risk Treatment Recommendations

- R-001: Implement phishing awareness training
  and multifactor authentication.
- R-002: Apply least-privilege access controls
  and periodic access reviews.
- R-003: Require independent verification
  of financial payment instructions.
- R-004: Implement endpoint protection,
  timely patching, and security monitoring.
- R-005: Maintain protected backups and
  regularly test recovery procedures.
