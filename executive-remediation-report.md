# Executive Cybersecurity Risk Remediation Report

## Maryknoll Sisters — Independent Educational Simulation

**Report Date:** November 25, 2026

**Framework:** NIST CSF 2.0

**Report Status:** Proposed — Educational Simulation

## 1. Executive Summary

This simulated cybersecurity risk assessment
identified five hypothetical risks affecting
a faith-based nonprofit environment.

The assessment recommends prioritized security
controls to protect organizational information,
financial resources, and operational continuity.

## 2. Risk Assessment Summary

| Risk Severity | Number of Risks |
|---|---|
| Critical | 1 |
| High | 3 |
| Medium | 1 |
| Low | 0 |
| Total | 5 |

## 3. Priority Recommendations

- Strengthen financial transaction verification
  and multifactor authentication.
- Implement cybersecurity awareness training.
- Enforce least-privilege access and periodic
  user access reviews.
- Maintain protected backups and test recovery.
- Strengthen endpoint protection, application
  allowlisting, and patch management.

## 4. Proposed Remediation Timeline

- Within 15 days: Address critical risks.
- Within 30 days: Address high-priority risks.
- Within 60 days: Address medium-priority risks.

Timelines begin upon hypothetical approval.

## 5. Management Responsibilities

- Approve proposed remediation priorities.
- Assign accountable risk and control owners.
- Allocate appropriate cybersecurity resources.
- Review remediation progress regularly.
- Require supporting evidence before closing
  remediation actions.

## 6. Conclusion

The proposed recommendations support responsible
governance, risk reduction, information protection,
and organizational resilience.

Residual risks should be reassessed following
verified control implementation.
