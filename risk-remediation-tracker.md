# Cybersecurity Risk Remediation Tracker

## Maryknoll Sisters — Independent Educational Simulation

**Framework:** NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

**NIST Mapping:** ID.RA-06 — Risk responses are chosen, prioritized, planned, tracked, and communicated.

**Status:** Proposed — Educational Simulation

## 1. Purpose

This tracker documents hypothetical cybersecurity
risks, remediation actions, responsible roles,
proposed completion timelines, and progress.

The objective is to demonstrate structured
cybersecurity risk treatment and accountability.

## 3. Remediation Status Definitions

- Proposed: Recommended action awaiting approval.
- In Progress: Approved remediation work has started.
- Pending Verification: Implementation awaits evidence review.
- Completed: Control implementation has been verified.
- Deferred: Remediation postponed with documented justification.

## 4. Monitoring and Reporting

- Review remediation progress weekly.
- Document evidence before closing actions.
- Escalate overdue critical and high-priority risks.
- Reassess residual risk after controls are implemented.
- Report remediation status to senior management.

## 5. Proposed Evidence

- MFA configuration and access review reports.
- Cybersecurity awareness training records.
- Account provisioning and deactivation logs.
- Backup restoration test reports.
- Patch management and endpoint security reports.

## 6. Management Accountability

The hypothetical management team is responsible
for approving remediation plans, assigning
appropriate resources, and monitoring progress.

All target timelines begin upon simulated
management approval.

## 7. Disclaimer

This is an independent educational cybersecurity
simulation inspired by Maryknoll Sisters.

All identified risks, assigned roles, timelines,
remediation activities, and status values are
hypothetical.

This document is not an actual assessment or
official remediation plan of Maryknoll Sisters.

No confidential organizational information or
