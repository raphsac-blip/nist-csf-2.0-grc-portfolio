# Cybersecurity Asset Inventory

## Maryknoll Sisters — Simulated Case Study

### Purpose

This asset inventory identifies hypothetical
information systems, technology resources, and
data assets within a simulated faith-based
nonprofit environment.

### Framework Alignment

NIST CSF 2.0: Identify (ID)
Category: Asset Management (ID.AM)

### Criticality Definitions

- Critical: Loss or compromise could cause severe
  operational, financial, or security consequences.
- High: Loss or compromise could significantly
  disrupt operations or expose sensitive information.
- Medium: Loss or compromise could cause moderate
  disruption with manageable consequences.
- Low: Loss or compromise would have limited
  organizational impact.

### Disclaimer

This inventory is entirely hypothetical and
developed for educational purposes. It does not
represent the actual systems, assets, or security
infrastructure of Maryknoll Sisters.

No confidential organizational information is used.
